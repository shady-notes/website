## The short version

**Your notes never leave your Mac.** They are not uploaded, synced, indexed or
read by us. There is no account requirement to write notes, and no telemetry in
the editor.

## What does go online

One thing: an **account token**, sent to `api.shadynotes.app` to check whether
your plan is active.

That request carries the token and nothing else — no note content, no filenames,
no vault paths, no usage statistics.

It happens when you sign in, and periodically afterwards. Between checks the app
works offline for 14 days.

:::note
This is a deliberately precise claim. Shady Notes is not a "nothing ever leaves
your machine" app — it does contact a server to verify entitlement. What it does
not do is send anything about your notes.
:::

## Two other network connections

- **Update checks**, once a day, to the release feed. A version request; nothing
  about you. → [Updates](/account/updates/)
- **Sign-in**, which happens in your browser against our authentication
  provider. The app never sees your password.

## Where things are stored

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Notes and attachments | Your vault folder, as plain files |
| Account token | The macOS Keychain |
| Settings, workspaces, starred notes | macOS preferences |
| Revision history | A git repository on your Mac |

All local. Nothing is on a server.

## If you never sign in

The app works. You get the free tier plus a 14-day trial, and the only network
traffic is the daily update check.

## Deleting your data

Notes are yours: delete the folder. Sign out to remove the token from the
Keychain. Uninstalling removes the app and leaves your notes alone.
