## What is gated

Exactly **three** features need a trial or a paid plan. Everything else in Shady
Notes is free, permanently, with no note limit and no nagging.

| Premium | Free |
|---|---|
| [Plugins](/plugins/overview/) | Everything else |
| All 16 [themes](/customising/themes/) | Dark, Light and Nord |
| [Revision history](/history/revisions/) | — |

Writing, the shade, the vault, [workspaces](/concepts/workspaces/),
[tabs](/organising/tabs/), the [Navigator](/organising/navigator/),
[sticky notes](/organising/sticky-notes/), search, find and replace,
[conflict handling](/history/conflicts/), every Markdown feature, all
[keyboard shortcuts](/reference/keyboard-shortcuts/) and every setting are free.

## The trial

Fourteen days, starting the first time the app checks — no sign-up, no card, no
button to press. During it, all three gated features are unlocked.

<Image src={imgPrefsPremiumFree} alt="The Premium tab in Preferences showing days remaining in the trial." />

When it ends the app returns to the free tier. **Nothing is taken away that you
made:** notes are untouched, plugin-managed notes stay exactly as they are, and
recorded history is kept — you simply stop being able to browse it until you are
on a paid plan again.

If your theme was a premium one, it falls back to a free one.

:::caution
Trial state is stored **per Mac**, not per account. Installing on a second
computer starts a separate trial there, and it is not restored by signing in.
:::

## The plans

Prices and the full comparison are on
[shadynotes.app/pricing](https://shadynotes.app/pricing). In short:

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | What it adds |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | $0 | The editor, the vault, everything not listed above |
| **Personal** | $4 | $36 | All three premium features, and updates while subscribed |
| **Supporter** | $8 | $72 | Personal, plus priority support, early builds and a heavier vote on the roadmap |

Yearly saves 25%. Both paid plans unlock exactly the same three features —
Supporter is patronage rather than a bigger app.

It is a subscription; there is no one-time or lifetime purchase. Checkout runs
through **Clerk Billing**, with Stripe as the payment processor. Round Rect is
the seller of record and handles tax, and you manage or cancel the subscription
from your account at any time.

Refunds are **14 days, no questions** — email `hello@shadynotes.app` from the
address on your receipt. → [Refund policy](https://shadynotes.app/refund)

## Signing in

Buying happens on the web; the app finds out about it when you sign in. Subscribe
at [shadynotes.app/pricing](https://shadynotes.app/pricing), then sign in from
**Preferences → Premium** and the app picks up the entitlement on its next check.
→ [Signing in](/account/sign-in/)

## Offline

Once entitlement is established, Shady Notes works offline for **14 days**
before it needs to check again. A flight, or a week without a connection, is not
a problem.

## Cancelling

If a subscription lapses, the app returns to the free tier at the next check.
Your notes are never touched by any licensing state.

## What goes online

Only the app token, and only to check your account. **Your notes never leave your
Mac** — they are not uploaded, synced or read by us. → [Privacy and
data](/help/privacy-and-data/)
