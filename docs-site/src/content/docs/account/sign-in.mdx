## How it works

Sign-in happens in your browser, not in the app — the app never sees a password.

1. **Preferences → Premium → Sign in**
2. Your browser opens `shadynotes.app/app/connect`
3. Sign in or create an account there
4. The page hands back to Shady Notes and the browser can be closed

<Image src={imgSigninConnectPage} alt="The sign-in card that /app/connect redirects to when signed out, offering GitHub, Google or email." />

The app receives a token, stores it in the **macOS Keychain**, and checks your
entitlement.

<Image src={imgPrefsPremiumSignedIn} alt="The Premium tab showing trial status, subscription controls and the revision history switch." />

## Signed in is not the same as subscribed

Two independent things:

- **Signed in** — a token is stored and the app knows which account you are
- **Entitled** — that account currently has an active plan

You can be signed in with no subscription — during the trial, or on the free
tier, that is the normal state. Subscribing happens on the web rather than in
the app, and once a plan is on the account the app picks it up on its next check
without signing in again.

## Working offline

After a successful check, the app works offline for **14 days** before needing
to re-verify.

## Signing out

**Preferences → Premium → Sign out** removes the token from the Keychain and
returns the app to the free tier. Notes are untouched.

## If sign-in does not complete

- Make sure the browser actually finished loading the connect page
- The hand-off uses a `shadynotes://` link — allow the browser to open it
- If the browser landed on the page but nothing happened in the app, close the
  tab and start again from Preferences; the link is single-use
