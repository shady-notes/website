Direct DMG

The straight path: download the signed, notarised disk image, drag Shady Notes into /Applications, and launch. No installer, no helper apps, no kernel extensions.

Light footprint

A native AppKit + SwiftUI app with no bundled browser and no external packages. It sits in your menu bar and stays out of the way until you scroll.

Your vault, ready

First launch creates ~/Documents/ShadyNotes/ (or point it at an existing Obsidian vault in Settings). Notes are plain .md files from the first keystroke.

Requirements.

  • macOS 13 Ventura or later — Apple Silicon and Intel, both served by the same universal download.
  • About a scroll wheel's worth of muscle memory. That's it.
  • No account, no sign-up: the trial starts when the app does.
