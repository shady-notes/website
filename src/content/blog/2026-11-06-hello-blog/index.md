Shady Notes now has a blog. It is where release notes get more room than a
changelog line, where how-tos walk through one workflow at a time, and where we
explain why the app works the way it does.

## What to expect

- **Release news:** what changed in a version and how to update.
- **How-to:** one task, start to finish, in the current version.
- **Behind the scenes:** the reasoning behind a design decision.

## Follow along

Add the feed to your reader:

```
https://shadynotes.app/blog/feed.xml
```

Or [download Shady Notes](https://shadynotes.app/download) and see for yourself.
