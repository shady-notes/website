Shady Notes lives above your screen, out of the way until you want it. There
are four ways to bring it down, and you can use whichever suits the moment.

## 1. Scroll near the menu bar

Move the pointer to the top of the screen and scroll. The shade slides down
over whatever you are doing. Scroll the other way and it goes back up.

The direction is configurable, and Shady Notes detects whether you are using a
mouse or a trackpad, so the gesture feels natural on both.

## 2. Use the global hotkey

Press ⌃⌥⌘↓ (Control-Option-Command-Down) from inside any app to toggle the
shade. You do not need to switch windows, use the Dock or press ⌘Tab first.

If that shortcut clashes with another app, rebind it to one you prefer.

## 3. Click the pull tab

While Shady Notes runs, a small amber tab hangs below the menu bar. Click it to
toggle the shade. When your pointer moves away, the tab shrinks to a quiet dot
so it does not get in the way.

## 4. Pin it, or make it a window

Sometimes you want your notes to stay put:

- **Pin** the shade open while you copy something from another app.
- **Detach** it into a regular window you can move and resize.
- **Hide the chrome** for a distraction-free writing surface.

## Which to use

Most people end up with two: the scroll for a quick glance, and the hotkey when
their hands are on the keyboard. Try each for a day and keep the ones you reach
for.
