The **Navigator** can keep several notes open at once as tabs. Open a note in a
new tab, and a tab strip appears above the preview, with a close button on each
tab. This arrived in v4.0.21.

## Open a note in a new tab

- **⌥-click** a note to open it in a new tab.
- Or **right-click** the note and open it in a new tab from the menu.
- From the keyboard, press **⌃⌥→** to open the selected note in a new tab.

A plain click still opens the note in the current tab, so nothing changes until
you ask for a new one.

## Move between tabs and close them

- **⌃⇥** cycles through your open tabs.
- **⌃⌥W** closes the current tab.
- Or click the close button on the tab itself.

## Narrow the list with workspace filters

If your vault has several workspaces, **⌥Tab** steps through the filters: All,
then Root, then each workspace in turn, and back to All. **⇧⌥Tab** goes the
other way. It is a quick way to find a note in one project without the rest of
the vault in the list.

## Try it

Pick two notes you often switch between, such as a meeting note and the project
it belongs to, and open both as tabs. Then use ⌃⇥ to flip between them.
