Shady Notes comes with sixteen themes: eleven dark and five light. They include
Dracula, Nord, Gruvbox, Tokyo Night, Rosé Pine, Solarized and four Catppuccin
flavours. You can switch between them without opening a settings window.

## Open the quick picker

Press ⌘⌥T. A list of themes appears. Hover over a theme and the shade previews
it straight away, so you can compare a few before you choose. Pick one to keep
it.

## Choosing one

A few things to try:

- **Match your editor.** If you use Nord, Dracula or a Catppuccin flavour in
  your code editor, use the same one here and the shade feels like part of the
  same desk.
- **Light for daytime.** Solarized Light, Catppuccin Latte and Gruvbox Light
  are easier to read in a bright room.
- **Dark for evenings.** Tokyo Night and Rosé Pine are low-contrast and quiet.

## Which themes are free

The Free tier includes three themes: Dark, Light and Nord. Every other theme is
part of the Personal and Supporter plans. During the 14-day trial on first
launch, all sixteen are unlocked, so you can try them all before deciding.
Plans and prices are on [the pricing page](https://shadynotes.app/pricing).
